Who's the Impostor?

Everyone in the room gets the same secret word… except the Impostor. Take turns giving one-word clues, watch for anyone who's bluffing, then vote to catch them before they talk their way out of it.

How to play

Four steps, every round

How to play

  1. 1

    Get your word

    Everyone secretly gets the same word… except one random player, the Impostor, who gets nothing.

  2. 2

    Give a clue

    Taking turns, say one short clue related to the word. The Impostor has to bluff along.

  3. 3

    Spot the impostor

    Listen for clues that feel vague or off. Someone doesn't actually know the word.

  4. 4

    Vote & reveal

    Vote for who you think is lying. Catch the Impostor before they steal the round back.

Scoring

  • Correct vote

    Crew who vote for the real Impostor earn 1 point.

  • Impostor escapes

    If the vote doesn't land on the Impostor, they earn 2 points.

  • Impostor steals it back

    Caught Impostors get one guess at the secret word. Right answer: still 2 points. Wrong: 0.

Caught the impostor but they guess the word correctly? They still walk away with 2 points.

Rules

  1. Clues can't contain the secret word itself, the game blocks it.
  2. Only the active player can give a clue, one at a time, in order.
  3. You can't vote for yourself, and votes lock in once cast.
