Team Escape

Two Teams. One Race. Escape Together.

Stack on your teammates, hold the gates, light the lanterns and carry the key home. Both teams race through identical ruins at the same time — the first team to get everyone through the door wins the round.

How to play

  1. 1

    Join a team

    Pick Team Moss or Team Ember in the lobby and ready up. Both teams need the same number of players.

  2. 2

    Work together

    Stack up to reach ledges, crowd the pressure plates, light every lantern at once and push crates as a group to reach the key.

  3. 3

    Escape first

    Carry the key to the door, unlock it, and get every teammate through before the other team does.

Controls

  • A / D · ← →

    Move. Walk into a crate with teammates to push it.

  • W · ↑ · Space

    Jump. Land on a teammate's head to stand on them.

  • E

    Light a lantern, or hand the key to a teammate next to you.

  • Esc

    Pause menu. On phones, on-screen buttons appear.

Rules

  1. Puzzles scale to your team size: 5-player teams get puzzles built for five.
  2. Fall in the brambles and you pop back at your team's last flag.
  3. Win a round: 1 point. Draw: half each. A tie after the last round goes to sudden death.
