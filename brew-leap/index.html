How to play Field guide

Three moves. That's the whole job.

How to play

  1. 1

    Hold to brew

    Press and hold anywhere, or hold Space. The meter fills, then drains again. Holding longer doesn't always mean jumping further.

  2. 2

    Release to leap

    Let go and your barista launches. More power means a longer, higher arc. Grab coffee beans on the way across.

  3. 3

    Stick the landing

    Land on the next cliff to score it. Come up short and you'll spill. Each spill costs a heart.

Read the signs

Field guide

Fair warning: the further you go, the wider the gaps and the narrower the cliffs.

Three hearts

Every run starts with three. Fall into a gap and you lose one.

Coffee beans

Some gaps have beans floating over them. Grab them mid-air to fill your stash.

Perfect patch

Touch down on the bright patch in the middle of a cliff for a bonus bean.
