The Name Game

A creative guessing game where players describe famous characters or people for others to guess. The challenge is to describe someone in a way that makes them recognizable without saying their name! Swap in your own Offshorly teammates for a round that's all about the people you work with.

Four steps, every round

How to play

  1. 1

    Choose a character

    Pick one of three famous characters or people to describe to the rest of the players.

  2. 2

    Describe them

    Write creative clues so that most, but not everyone, gets it.You're told how many people can guess correctly without going bust when the game starts.

  3. 3

    Guess the others

    Read each description and pick who you think it is from the list before the timer runs out.

  4. 4

    Score big

    You get more points for every person who guesses yours correctly… unless too many do!

Scoring

  • Points for guessing

    Earn a point every time you correctly guess a character.

  • Points for describing

    Earn a point for each player who guesses your character from your clues.

  • Don't BUST!

    If too many players guess your character, you bust and get no points.

Example: 9 players means 8 guessers. Up to 6 can get it; the 7th busts you.

Rules

  1. Don't give any indication of your answer before all are revealed.
  2. No saying the name. The game blocks any word from it in your clue.
  3. If it feels like cheating, it probably is. Don't do it!

Plays on any phone or laptop
